# r74m O3 -- Audit of the TOST/IUT Quality Battery, Margins, and Rung-4 vs 3Q Decision Logic

Dataset: `eval_output/fidelity_definitive_r73/per_combo.json` (3955 rows; 574 rung-4 -- matches the brief).
NOTE: the live `eval_output/fidelity_definitive/per_combo.json` is the STALE r70 build (705 rung-4,
no battery fields). All numbers below use the r73 build, which is the one with `battery_p_iut`,
`np_p_tost`, `cross_p_tost`, `quality_identical_raw`, etc.

Code: `scripts/definitive_fidelity_analysis.py` (battery construction + margins),
`dagua/eval/distributional_fidelity.py` (TOST primitives + `assign_rung`),
`scripts/definitive_fidelity_report.py` (BH families, controls gates, rung finalization).

================================================================================
## 1. HOW "QUALITY-IDENTICAL" (3Q) IS DECIDED
================================================================================

### The battery = intersection-union test (IUT) over 3 metrics
`compute_mode_a_quality_battery` (analysis.py:1139) and `compute_mode_b_quality_battery` (:1182)
run three paired (Mode A) / one-sample (Mode B) TOSTs:
  - normalized STRESS      (`df.paired_tost`, analysis.py:1167)
  - edge CROSSINGS         (:1171)
  - kNN NEIGHBORHOOD-PRESERVATION ("np", k=10) (:1175)

`quality_battery_record` (analysis.py:1312) combines them:
  - `battery_p_iut = max(stress_p, cross_p, np_p)` only if all 3 finite, else NaN  (analysis.py:1347-1351)
    -> IUT: ALL three TOSTs must reject => the conjunction p-value is the MAX (Berger-Hsu 1996).
  - `quality_identical_raw = stress_ok AND cross_ok AND np_ok`  (:1355), where
    `metric_equivalent` (analysis.py:1408) = `p_tost < 0.05` (or degenerate-SD direct pass).

### Multiple-comparison correction ACROSS combos
- WITHIN a battery: NONE (comment analysis.py:1348-1350 -- max-p conjunction is already level-alpha).
- ACROSS combos: Benjamini-Hochberg on the `battery_p_iut` family -> `q_battery`
  (`apply_bh_family(eligible, "battery_p_iut", "q_battery")`, report.py:436; `df.bh_fdr` at
  distributional_fidelity.py:312).

### The 3Q decision (assign_rung, distributional_fidelity.py:343)
```
quality_identical = record["quality_identical"]  (raw)   OR   q_battery < 0.05      (line 363-364)
```
Then in the Mode A branch (lines 366-391): 3Q is only REACHED when the combo is NOT already
rung 1/2 and NOT rung 3-by-stress; i.e. 3Q sits BELOW the distributional rungs and ABOVE rung 3/4:
  - if dist_equivalent and tracking -> "1"
  - elif dist_equivalent          -> "2"
  - elif quality_identical        -> "3Q"      <-- positions NOT equivalent, but battery passes
  - elif quality (stress-only)    -> "3"
  - else                          -> "4"
(`quality` at line 362 = `quality_equivalent` OR `q_tost < 0.05`, the LOOSER stress-only TOST.)

So the gate to 3Q is: `quality_identical_raw==True` OR BH-`q_battery < 0.05`. Empirically
`quality_identical_raw` carries it (the IUT max-p almost never survives BH when the raw is False).

## 2. THE MARGINS -- EXACT VALUES + CALIBRATION VERDICT
================================================================================

Constants (analysis.py:50-53):
```
QUALITY_STRESS_REL_MARGIN = 0.02
QUALITY_NP_ABS_MARGIN     = 0.02
QUALITY_CROSS_REL_MARGIN  = 0.02
QUALITY_CROSS_ABS_FLOOR   = 0.5
```
Per-metric margins:
  - STRESS:    `max(0.02 * mean(stress_R), 1e-6)`         (`quality_stress_margin`, analysis.py:1376-1389)
  - CROSSINGS: `max(0.02 * mean(cross_R), 0.5)`           (`quality_cross_margin`, analysis.py:1392-1405)
  - np:        `0.02` ABSOLUTE                            (constant fed directly, analysis.py:1177/1223)

(Deterministic-different engines use a parallel set: `deterministic_quality_identical`,
report.py:1155 -- stress_rel<0.02, np_delta<0.02, crossings_delta<=max(0.02*cross_R,0.5).
Same numbers, single-pair.)

### Are these absurdly tight vs the REFERENCE's own seed-to-seed variance?
This was the central calibration question. The honest answer is TWO-SIDED:

(a) The np ABSOLUTE 0.02 margin is the most suspect. np is a [0,1] preservation fraction; a 0.02
    absolute band is plausibly tighter than a stochastic reference's own 100-seed np spread for
    larger/looser graphs. BUT empirically np is NOT what's failing (see below), so its tightness is
    not the binding problem for rung-4.

(b) The STRESS 2% relative margin IS tight relative to reference self-variance for some combos,
    but stress failures are overwhelmingly driven by genuine MEAN SHIFTS, not by variance:

    Among the 570 rung-4 combos with battery stress data, the relative gap
    |mean(stress_D) - mean(stress_R)| / mean(stress_R):
       p10 = 4.5% | p25 = 28.0% | p50 = 99.8% | p75 = 100.0% | p90 = 194.3%
       within 2% (margin):  5.8%   |  within 5%: 10.9%  |  within 10%: 14.0%
    => the MEDIAN rung-4 combo has a ~100% relative stress difference. These are not FP-floor;
       they are genuinely, grossly different in drawing quality.

    Variance-only failures DO exist but are a small minority: of the 33 combos (5.8%) whose stress
    means are within margin, 11 (33%) still fail the stress TOST purely because the seed-to-seed
    SE exceeds the 2% margin. That is real mis-calibration -- but it touches ~11/570 ~= 2% of rung-4,
    not the bulk.

VERDICT: The margins are tight, and the np-absolute and stress-2% margins are arguably tighter than
the reference's own self-variance for a minority of combos (the variance-only failures, ~2-6%).
But the dominant cause of rung-4 is NOT margin mis-calibration -- it is large, genuine mean gaps
(median ~100% relative stress). The "FP floor" framing is therefore WRONG for the majority: most
rung-4 combos are genuinely quality-divergent. The brief's hypothesis ("genuine FP-floor must be
quality-indistinguishable; any quality difference = artifact or real cause") is upheld as a
principle, and the data resolve it in favor of "real cause" for ~86%+ of rung-4.

### CAVEAT -- calibration is UNVERIFIED by construction (taxonomy/control gap)
There is NO positive control that exercises the quality battery on genuinely-equivalent pairs.
Control gates (report.py:2242-2385):
  - gate_1 positive Mode A: checks rungs {0,1,2} only (DISTRIBUTIONAL) -- 39/39 pass, 100%.
  - gate_2 positive Mode B: checks {0,2'} only.
  - gate_5 anti-laundering: checks 3Q RATE <= 5% on NEGATIVE+chance controls (a FALSE-POSITIVE
    bound) -- passes (40 scored, 3Q rate within 5%).
NONE asserts "the battery PASSES (3Q) at rate >= X on ref-vs-ref-equivalent inputs." So the battery's
SENSITIVITY (its ability to certify a truly-equivalent layout as 3Q) is never validated. If the
margins were too tight to ever pass, no existing gate would catch it -- gate_5 would actually be
HAPPIER. This is the single most important construction risk in the battery.

## 3. BINDING CONSTRAINT -- O6 IS WRONG; STRESS BINDS, NOT np
================================================================================

Among the 570 rung-4 combos with a finite battery, which metric is the MAX-p (the one that fails the
IUT)?  (binding = argmax of the three p_tost)
       STRESS: 508 (89.1%)   |   CROSSINGS: 43 (7.5%)   |   np: 19 (3.3%)

Raw alpha-pass rate per metric (p_tost < 0.05) among rung-4:
       stress:  22/570 =  3.9%   <-- the bottleneck
       cross:  185/570 = 32.5%
       np:     275/570 = 48.2%   <-- np is the EASIEST to pass, opposite of O6's claim

O6 reported "kNN/np is the binding constraint." The r73 data flatly contradict this: STRESS is the
binding constraint in 89% of rung-4 combos; np passes nearly half the time. O6 likely inspected an
earlier build, or conflated "np absolute margin looks tightest on paper" with "np is what fails."
Recommend O6's binding-constraint finding be retracted/corrected.

Raw IUT pass at alpha=0.05 (no relaxation): 0/570 -- consistent with O6's "0/574 pass" headline, but
the REASON is stress, not np.

================================================================================
## 4. TAXONOMY GAP -- "positions differ but quality statistically equal"
================================================================================

There IS such a rung: 3Q. By construction (assign_rung, distributional_fidelity.py:378-391), 3Q is
reached ONLY when the combo is NOT distributionally position-equivalent (not rung 1/2) yet the full
quality battery passes. Confirmed in data: all 36 r73 3Q (mode-A) rows have dist_equivalent=False
(0/36 would have been rung-2). So 3Q == exactly "positions differ, quality identical."

So the taxonomy is NOT missing the category -- 3Q fills it. The real gap is QUANTITATIVE, not
categorical: 3Q is so hard to reach (stress 2% + np 0.02 + cross max(2%,0.5), all via TOST) that it
captures only 36 combos repo-wide. rung-4 is therefore NOT conflating "positionally divergent" with
"quality-divergent" in PRINCIPLE -- the data show most rung-4 ARE quality-divergent (median ~100%
stress gap). But for the ~2-14% tail with near-matched means, the binary 3Q-or-4 cliff dumps
genuinely quality-close-but-noisy combos into rung-4 with no intermediate "quality-equivalent but
not bit-identical-quality" label. (rung 3 partially serves this -- it's the LOOSER stress-only
quality-equivalent tier, 279 combos -- but rung 3 ignores cross/np entirely.)

RECOMMENDATION: if a "FP-floor / quality-indistinguishable" bucket is wanted, define it via
reference-self-variance-tied margins (e.g. stress margin = k * within-reference stress SD, np margin =
k * within-reference np SD) and add a POSITIVE control that the battery 3Q-passes ref-vs-ref pairs at
>=90%. Without the positive control the margins cannot be defended either way.

================================================================================
## 5. DEFENSIBLE-MARGIN PASS ESTIMATE (pre-controls)
================================================================================

Method: exact observed metric means (battery_stress/cross/np_D_mean vs _R_mean), test the NECESSARY
condition |meanD - meanR| < margin for all three (intersection). This UPPER-BOUNDS true TOST passes
(TOST additionally requires small SE; variance can only reduce the count). Validation: at the current
2% margin this upper bound = 8/570, and the actual raw IUT pass = 0/570 -- consistent (the gap is the
variance term).

   Margins (stress/cross/np)      full-battery passes (UPPER BOUND)   per-metric (stress|cross|np)
   ----------------------------   ---------------------------------   ----------------------------
   CURRENT  2% / 2% / 0.02         8 / 570  (1.4%)                      33 | 223 | 297
   DEFENSIBLE 5% / 5% / 0.05      33 / 570  (5.8%)                      62 | 282 | 444
   LOOSE   10% / 10% / 0.10       47 / 570  (8.2%)                      80 | 352 | 490
   ABSURD  20% / 20% / 0.10       83 / 570  (14.6%)                    121 | 447 | 490

CONCLUSION: Even at a defensible 5% margin the battery would reclassify only ~33 (UPPER bound; true
TOST count lower) of the 574 rung-4 as quality-identical. At a loose 10% margin, ~47 (8.2%). The
remaining ~86-92% of rung-4 have genuinely divergent drawing quality (stress gaps far exceeding any
defensible margin) and are correctly NOT FP-floor. The "FP floor" explanation for the bulk of rung-4
is FALSEHOOD; only a ~2-8% tail is plausibly margin-calibration noise.

Of the 80 rung-4 combos whose stress is within 10%, the remaining blockers are: cross >=10% for 29,
np >=0.10 for 4 -- so once stress is relaxed, CROSSINGS becomes the secondary binding metric, not np.

## SUMMARY OF VERDICTS
================================================================================
1. 3Q = (quality_identical_raw OR BH q_battery<0.05); battery = IUT max-p over stress/cross/np;
   within-battery no correction, across-combo BH on battery_p_iut. (analysis.py:1347-1355,
   distributional_fidelity.py:362-391, report.py:436)
2. Margins: stress max(2%*meanR,1e-6); cross max(2%*meanR,0.5); np ABS 0.02. Tight; the np-absolute
   and stress-2% margins are tighter than reference self-variance for a ~2-6% minority (variance-only
   failures), but NOT the dominant cause of rung-4 (median rung-4 stress gap ~100%). Calibration is
   UNVERIFIED -- no positive control exercises the battery on equivalent pairs.
3. O6's "np is binding" is WRONG for r73: STRESS binds 89% (508/570); np is easiest (48% raw-pass).
4. Taxonomy: 3Q already means "positions differ, quality identical" (36 combos, all dist_equiv=False).
   No categorical gap; the gap is the harsh binary 3Q/4 cliff + absence of self-variance-tied margins.
5. Defensible-margin pass estimate: 5% -> ~33/570 (<=5.8%, upper bound); 10% -> ~47/570 (<=8.2%).
   ~86-92% of rung-4 are genuinely quality-divergent, NOT FP-floor.
