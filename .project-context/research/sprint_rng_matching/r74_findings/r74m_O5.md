# r74m_O5 — Empirical ground-truth adjudication of "FP floor" rung-4 combos

**Date:** 2026-06-22 · **Scope:** READ-ONLY. Recomputed metrics on stored 100-seed
layout clouds. **Reference cloud = same reference algorithm re-run with 100 seeds**
(`eval_output/benchmark_100seed_seeded_refs/`), the correct control for FP-floor.

## TL;DR verdict

The "FP floor" label on the rung-4 divergent combos is **mostly correct but NOT
universally** — and the *reason* these combos sit at rung 4 is almost never a
systematic dagua quality deficit. Two distinct mechanisms, plus one real localized bug:

1. **gem (n=19 clean rung-4): dagua is UNIFORMLY BETTER.** 0/19 worse, 19/19 better
   on stress (median delta -0.022), and better on np (+0.019). These are rung-4 purely
   because the quality battery's **2% relative margin is ~50-100x tighter than gem's
   reference seed-to-seed noise** — the test is far too tight, not dagua being worse.
   **Verdict: ARTIFACT (test margin), not real degradation. Not "more work" on dagua.**

2. **sfdp as a family (n=127): balanced FP-floor.** 63 worse / 64 better, mean stress
   delta +0.00013, median -0.0009. Genuine rounding-basin floor in aggregate.
   **EXCEPTION — `classic_sfdp_p_neg2` is a REAL localized signal** (see §4).

3. **fmmm (n=67): balanced/dagua-favoring** after using post-fix data (median +0.0007,
   np -0.01). Mostly floor; offsets sit inside reference noise. ARTIFACT-leaning.

## CRITICAL methodology finding (data-freshness trap)

The fidelity pipeline loads layouts via `load_results_multi` across MANY benchmark dirs;
**the newest mtime wins per combo.** A naive "load from escalation_final" gives STALE
pre-fix layouts for engines re-run later:
- gem reimpl current dir = `benchmark_100seed_gem_realfix` (Jun 12), NOT escalation_final (Jun 5).
- fmmm reimpl current dir = `benchmark_100seed_r73_fixes` (Jun 15).
- sfdp reimpl current dir = `escalation_final` (Jun 5) — still current.

My first pass loaded stale gem/fmmm and reported dagua catastrophically worse
(gem cohen's d=+5.2). After loading the **newest** dir per combo (matching
`load_results_multi`), the numbers flipped and reconciled to per_combo.json exactly.
**Any future verifier MUST resolve newest-dir-per-combo or it will fabricate regressions.**
(Recommend the project expose the resolved positions_file path per combo so verifiers
don't reverse-engineer it.)

## §2 Artifact vs real, per metric/mechanism

- **Battery never passes despite balanced/favorable quality.** Across all 224 clean
  mode-A connected rung-4 combos: `dist_equivalent=False` (100%), `quality_identical=False`
  (100%), `q_battery<0.05` for **0**. So rung-4 is set by the **Procrustes-basin distance
  test (`dist_equivalent`)** failing — exactly what FP-floor predicts (same algo, different
  basin) — NOT by a quality verdict. The quality battery then can't *rescue* them to 3Q
  because its margin is too tight relative to reference variance.

- **The 2% relative battery margin is the binding artifact.** For gem, stress margin
  ~0.0004-0.0012 while the REFERENCE's own seed-to-seed sd is 0.04-0.06 (margin is
  ~50-100x smaller than ref noise). Even a *better* dagua cloud cannot be declared
  "quality-identical" because the equivalence band is narrower than the reference's
  intrinsic spread. **This is the project lead's "test too tight" case, confirmed
  quantitatively (#3 in brief): the per-combo equivalence FAILURE is driven by the
  reference's own variance exceeding the margin, NOT a real offset — for gem/fmmm.**

- **Scale/aspect artifact ruled out.** Stress (closed-form optimal scale alpha) and np
  (rank-based knn) are scale-invariant by construction; recomputing after Procrustes does
  not change them. The gaps are not normalization/aspect artifacts.

## §3 The one REAL systematic difference: sfdp on specific graphs

`asymmetric_hourglass_hub / sfdp_default`: dagua stress **+0.0034 (+13% worse),
cohen's d=1.31, 93% of seeds worse, offset OUTSIDE the reference's 95% seed noise.**
This is NOT rounding chaos — it is a reproducible quality gap on this graph topology.
BUT sfdp *as a family* is balanced (63 worse / 64 better), so this is **per-graph**, not
a global sfdp regression. dagua-worse graphs cluster on: weighted_chain_20, asymmetric_
hourglass_hub, real_lesmis_77, compound_10x20, clustered_medium_5x20.

## §4 Suspected algorithmic cause — `classic_sfdp_p_neg2`

Per-variant sfdp stress-delta breakdown (clean rung-4):

| variant | n | mean delta | median | sd | notably-worse |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sfdp_default | 12 | -0.0008 | -0.0009 | 0.0054 | 5 |
| sfdp_graphviz_fidelity | 12 | -0.0008 | -0.0009 | 0.0054 | 5 |
| sfdp_theta04 | 12 | -0.0009 | -0.0009 | 0.0052 | 5 |
| sfdp_theta08 | 11 | -0.0008 | -0.0024 | 0.0061 | 4 |
| sfdp_steps200 | 16 | -0.0006 | +0.0002 | 0.0047 | 6 |
| **sfdp_p_neg2** | **64** | **+0.0010** | -0.0012 | **0.0118** | **27** |

`p_neg2` has **2.3x the stress-delta variance of every other sfdp variant** and the only
positive mean — a bimodal, parameter-sensitive failure, not a floor. p_neg2 sets the
repulsive exponent **p = -2** vs the default `_DEFAULT_P = -1.0`
(`dagua/layout/ops/pipelines/sfdp.py:51`). With p=-2 the supernode repulsive force
`~1/d^2` (`graphviz_supernode_repulsive_force` in `dagua/layout/ops/quadtree.py`, applied
via `SFDPAdaptiveCool` in `dagua/layout/ops/sfdp.py`) decays far faster, so the
adaptive-cooling schedule and per-level convergence become much more init-sensitive —
producing high bidirectional stress variance on graphs where the basin is fragile.

**Suspected cause + files (the "more work" signal):**
- `dagua/layout/ops/sfdp.py` — `SFDPAdaptiveCool` / `SFDPAdaptiveCoolConfig` cooling
  schedule and convergence tolerance (`_SFDP_FORCE_NORM_KEY` stopping criterion) likely
  not matched to graphviz for the p=-2 force law; the schedule that converges for p=-1
  under-/over-cools for p=-2.
- `dagua/layout/ops/quadtree.py` — `graphviz_supernode_repulsive_force`: verify the p=-2
  repulsive term (and its short-range `_GRAPHVIZ_MIN_DISTANCE` clamp) bit-matches
  graphviz's `Cexp/dist^p` and that quadtree theta approximation error isn't amplified
  at p=-2.

Recommended next step: rerun `classic_sfdp_p_neg2` with reference params mirrored
(same maxiter/theta/p) and per-seed Procrustes RMSD vs `graphviz_sfdp` to confirm it is
a convergence/force-law mismatch and not basin chaos. The other 5 sfdp variants, all
gem, and fmmm need NO further dagua work — they are test-margin artifacts / genuine floor.
