## Fix Status

- A cluster-aware compaction/order: kept. The effective winning part is conservative cluster-contiguous ordering plus cluster-aware x compaction for directed layered cluster classes. Single-cluster dependency DAGs get an internal unclustered pseudo-group so the explicit cluster separates from the rest of the graph.
- B rank-row snapping: kept with a shallow-DAG guard (`min_layers=10`) to avoid protected wide-DAG regressions.
- C component tiling scorer: registered and wired after component polish, but produced no measured movement on `disconnected_encoder_residual`; no claim is made for this target.
- D tiny multiedge cap: predicate kept but default cap neutralized at the existing 240-unit scale and skipped for clustered graphs after lower caps regressed protected/target composites.
