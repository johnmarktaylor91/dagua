**Critical Issues**
1. `CRITICAL` | Part 2 graph-relative ranking
What is wrong: the plan’s higher-is-better normalization is mathematically wrong for real metrics. It defines `graph_rel_best` as `best_value / value` when `value > eps`, else `inf`, but one primary metric, `depth_spearman_rho`, can be negative or zero, and `dag_consistency` can legitimately be zero. That makes the normalized score sign-flip or blow up, which corrupts family ranking and Pareto placement.
What should change: replace the higher-better formula with a gap-to-best transform that handles zero and negatives, or fall back to rank-only aggregation for those metrics. Add explicit tests for `depth_spearman_rho <= 0` and `dag_consistency == 0`.
